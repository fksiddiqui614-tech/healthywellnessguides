If advertising, analytics, embedded content, or other third-party services are added in the future, those providers may process information according to their own policies. We will make reasonable efforts to keep this Privacy Policy current as the website evolves.

6. Data retention and security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, legal requirements, dispute resolution, and legitimate business needs. 7. Your privacy choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding access, correction, deletion, portability, objection, or restriction of certain personal information. Requests can be made through our Contact page. We may need to verify a request before acting on it.

8. Children

This website is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. International visitors

Our website may be accessed from countries outside the country where our services are hosted. By using the website, you understand that information may be processed in jurisdictions with different data-protection laws.

We may update this Privacy Policy when our practices or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
