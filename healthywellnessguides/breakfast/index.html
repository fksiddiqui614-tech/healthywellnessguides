Look beyond the label.

Understanding calories, macros, sugar and ingredients can make everyday food decisions easier.

Small breakfast details can be easy to overlook

You do not need to label a food as “good” or “bad” to make a more informed choice. Start by understanding what is in it.

🍬

Added sugar

Some breakfast foods contain added sugars that can be easy to miss when you only look at the front of the package.

🥚

Protein matters

Eggs, Greek yogurt and other protein-rich foods can be useful building blocks for a more balanced breakfast.

📊

Nutrition is more than calories

Calories are only one part of the picture. Macros, sugar and other nutrition information can provide additional context.

A simple 3-step approach

Use nutrition information as context rather than trying to judge an entire diet from a single meal.

1

Look at the meal

Start with the foods you already eat instead of completely changing your routine.

2

Understand the numbers

3

Make informed choices

Use the information to compare meals and build choices that fit your own goals and preferences.
